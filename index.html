How I work

Simulate, prove, deploy

  1. 1

    Build the world

    A physics-accurate twin of the robot, its sensors and the place it will work: Isaac Sim, Gazebo or O3DE.

  2. 2

    Prove it in numbers

    Automated missions with pass/fail metrics, such as coverage, cross-track error and false positives, rerun after every change.

  3. 3

    Carry it to hardware

    The same ROS 2 stack and launch files move to the real robot, with fewer surprises in the field.
